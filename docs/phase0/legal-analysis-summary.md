## Findings summary

**Desk-research findings from public sources — not a counsel opinion.** Source index: [CMA Acts](https://cmauganda.co.ug/acts/), [CMA Regulations](https://cmauganda.co.ug/regulations/), [CMA Issuer/Regulatorinformation](https://cmauganda.co.ug/).

- **Instrument classification / offer process:** offers of securities are governed by the Capital Markets Authority Act (Cap. 64) and the **CMA (Offer of Securities) Regulations 2025**; book building by the Capital Markets (Book Building) Regulations 2016. Instrument form is addressed by the CMA (Prescription of Securities Instrument) Regulations 2022.
- **Custody:** the Securities Central Depositories Act (Cap. 60) and Securities Central Depositories Regulations 2009 govern central depositories. CMA reports 2 licensed CSDs and 2 approved stock exchanges.
- **Operating roles:** the Capital Markets (Licensing and Approval) Regulations 2025 govern licensing. CMA reports licensed 6 brokers, 14 dealers, 18 fund managers, 11 CIS managers, 2 trustees and 16 investment advisors. Which of these roles the platform, placement and custody functions map to is **not** settled by these sources.
- **Proposed first structure (desk hypothesis):** an equity offer under the 2025 Offer of Securities Regulations, with depository custody and a token layer used only as a representation of the registered security. This requires counsel confirmation before it is treated as viable.
- **Investor protection / marketing / reporting:** CMA (Conduct of Business) Regulations 2025, Capital Markets (Corporate Governance) Regulations 2025, CMA (Investor Compensation Fund) Regulations 2023, and CMA (Accounting and Financial Requirements) Regulations 2022.
- **Company register:** CMA lists the Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB) among its partner bodies; the exact company-law process for tokenized equity was not verified from primary legislation in this pass.

**Open for qualified counsel:** token classification; exact register/company-law process; platform/placement/custody/payment licensing mapping; payment, FX and stablecoin permissions; tax treatment. No MVP scope change is proposed here.
